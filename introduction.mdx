I build product marketing teams — from the ground up or by leveling up existing ones. My work spans:

- **Product launches** — orchestrating cross-functional launches that drive awareness and adoption
- **Conference & event production** — running developer and marketing conferences at scale
- **Team building** — recruiting, mentoring, and leading world-class PMM teams
